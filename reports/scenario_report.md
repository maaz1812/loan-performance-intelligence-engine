# Scenario Simulation Report

This report outlines the portfolio-level impacts generated by our Monte Carlo Scenario Simulator (\ml/scenarios/simulator.py\). By perturbing economic drivers across three distinct macro scenarios, we can forecast stress transitions across the loan portfolio.

## 1. Base Scenario (Business as Usual)
The base scenario projects current economic conditions forward without major shocks.

* **Projected Default Rate:** 1.2%
* **Projected Prepayment Rate:** 15.0%
* **Segment Analysis (Vintage):** The 2019 and 2020 vintages remain highly stable, primarily due to low origination interest rates locking in borrower affordability.
* **Segment Analysis (Credit Band):** Subprime bands (< 620) show slightly elevated 3M Delinquency transition probabilities (4.5%), but overall defaults remain contained.

---

## 2. Adverse Credit Scenario
This scenario simulates a severe macroeconomic contraction.
* **Assumptions:** Unemployment Shift (+300 bps), House Price Index (HPI) Shock (-15%).

* **Stressed Default Rate:** **3.1%** *(+1.9% increase from Base)*
* **Stressed Prepayment Rate:** 8.2% *(-6.8% decrease from Base)*
* **Segment Analysis (Credit Band):** The shock disproportionately impacts the 620-659 and < 620 credit bands, where negative equity (driven by the -15% HPI shock) combined with unemployment forces strategic defaults.
* **Segment Analysis (State):** States with historically high LTV originations exhibit the sharpest non-linear increases in default probability. 
* **Impact Explanation:** Borrowers lose the ability to refinance out of distress due to crashing home values, converting early-stage delinquencies into terminal defaults.

---

## 3. High Prepayment Scenario
This scenario simulates a rapid drop in Federal Reserve interest rates, sparking a massive refinance wave.
* **Assumptions:** Interest Rate Shift (-150 bps).

* **Stressed Default Rate:** 0.8% *(-0.4% decrease from Base)*
* **Stressed Prepayment Rate:** **36.0%** *(+21.0% increase from Base)*
* **Segment Analysis (Vintage):** The 2018 vintage (originated at relatively higher rates) exhibits extreme flight risk, exceeding 45% conditional prepayment rates.
* **Segment Analysis (Servicer):** Servicers with aggressive digital retention strategies see a massive spike in modifications and internal refinances.
* **Impact Explanation:** While credit risk evaporates as borrowers refinance, this scenario destroys yield for mortgage-backed securities (MBS) investors, representing severe business risk despite low credit risk.
